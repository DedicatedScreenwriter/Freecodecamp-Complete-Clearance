About Stardew Valley

Stardew Valley is an open-ended country-life RPG developed by Eric "ConcernedApe" Barone, who also created the game's pixel art, music, and programming. It was released for Microsoft Windows in February 2016, followed by macOS and Linux in July 2016, PlayStation 4 in December 2016, Xbox One in September 2017, Nintendo Switch in October 2017, PlayStation Vita in May 2018, iOS in October 2018, and Android in February 2020. The game was inspired by the Harvest Moon series and has sold over 20 million copies worldwide as of March 2021.

Gameplay

In Stardew Valley, players take on the role of a character who inherits their grandfather's dilapidated farm in a place known as Stardew Valley. Players can engage in various activities such as farming, raising animals, fishing, mining, foraging, and socializing with the townspeople. The game features a day-night cycle and changing seasons, each with its own set of crops and events. Players can also get married and start a family.

Development

Eric Barone began developing Stardew Valley in 2012 as a solo project after being disappointed with the direction of the Harvest Moon series. He spent four years working on the game, handling all aspects of development himself. Barone drew inspiration from various sources, including classic RPGs like The Legend of Zelda and simulation games like Animal Crossing.

Reception

Stardew Valley received critical acclaim upon its release, with praise directed at its charming pixel art style, addictive gameplay loop, and depth of content. The game has been lauded for its relaxing atmosphere and the freedom it offers players to pursue their own goals. It has won several awards and has been included in numerous "best of" lists for indie games.
